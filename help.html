Welcome to the help page! Here you can get help on various topics, and access the Not So Frequently Asked Questions.

Questions That Might Be Asked


What is this website?


It is more of a documentation hosted with GitHub Pages, so it's like a website, but smaller.
It contains basic info and some more detailed things here and there, and maybe some fun too.



What is frequently updated?


Everything is updated here and there, unless a major change needs to happen, so mostly Thingamajigs, and it's addons are priority. New or updated games will have their pages updated quickly as well as mods. Pages that are not gaming or modding-related may be updated occasionally.



What does CMB use to make tech-related/real-life things with?


For tech things: IDEs, image manipulation programs, 3d model making programs, game-engines, and of course, the mind.

For real things: Paper, pencils, pens, anything that has air in it for storage, etc.; oh and of course, the mind.

Note: CMB at one point or another used Photoshop, Pixelmator, Affinity, and of course, GIMP.

CMB has used Blockbench, and even manual file modification of standard block models for modding in MC. For non-modding, Blender is the tool of choice.

Organization is done using table programs or even just paper or digital drawing plans and other things. Whatever program or real-life object that can organize things.

CMB recommends supporting free, (not freemium) apps as they are free for all. Avoid paid apps unless you are a business that actually needs the features they offer.

For paid apps, go one-time pay only (if CMB could make a suite of apps and actually keep it free forever without going bankrupt that would be nice).

You can use whatever app you want. Even if apps are recommended here or unrecommended, the best choice should always be the customers' choice.







Thingamajigs Help

In order to install classic Thingamajigs, you'll need Forge, specifically the one for each version cycle of the mod. The most up to date and popular for it's cycle is MC 1.20.1.


If installing Thingamajigs 2, Overpowered Mastery or CMATD, you'll need NeoForge. Usually this one updates to each major MC version (well almost). 1.21.1 is the most popular and well documented version. Hopefully newest and 1.21.1 versions will always be synced in terms of features. If not, be very patient, it may take months or longer to update (4+ different mod codebases to manage!).

Long Roads or Railroadways requires NeoForge and Thingamajigs 2, as it needs it as a base or 'core' mod to work (not required anymore as of 1.0.1 updates for both mods). These mods always try to update just after the main mod updates, as they are basically feature complete.

Mods may be ported to strange versions of MC, to see if anything major needs to be changed. If the mod compiles and works basically perfectly, a new version may release properly for that version of MC.

CMB does not abandon MC versions that are popular and have a strong following by players (unless unable to mod anymore due to external forces which we have no control over 😃).

Examples of versions that are quite good for modding: 1.20.1, 1.21.1. Tech, magic, decoration and biome mods are all on these versions, old and new. And of course, some of CMBs' mods are on 1.20.1 and 1.21.1.

Most new mods are on 1.21.1 because features that were split from Thingamajigs 1 turned into their own mods (to allow freedom of choice and less packed together content).
